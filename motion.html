How a website comes together.

Most sites take four to eight weeks. You see it on a live link the whole way, and nothing is a surprise at the end.

  1. 01 · Listen

    Start with the business.

    A conversation about who visits, what they need, and what they should do next. We agree the pages, the scope and the quote.

  2. 02 · Shape

    Words and a direction.

    A sitemap, the copy drafted with you, and a design direction in both lights before anything is built.

  3. 03 · Build

    Watch it take shape.

    Hand-coded, page by page, on a live link you can open any time. Quick on a phone from the first day.

  4. 04 · Launch

    Found from day one.

    Search set-up, share images, the marketing HQ, and the domain, hosting and accounts all in your name.

  5. 05 · Keep it alive

    Yours to run.

    A monthly hour from us, or we teach you the parts that change. Leave any time and take everything with you.

Five rules every site is built to.

The difference between a site that feels made and one that feels generated is care in the small things.

  1. It has a job.

    Every page points somewhere: what to read, where to look, what to do next.

  2. Quick on a phone.

    If it isn’t fast on a mid-range phone on a weak signal, it isn’t done.

  3. Found on Google.

    Search set-up is part of the build, not an extra.

  4. Works for everyone.

    Keyboard, screen reader, motion off: the same content and the same controls.

  5. One signature moment.

    One thing people remember. Everything around it stays quiet.

Questions people ask first.

How long does a website take?+

Four to eight weeks for most, depending on how many pages there are and how much is already written.

What does it cost?+

Quoted per project after a conversation about scope. We don’t publish invented prices.

Do you build on WordPress, Squarespace or Shopify?+

By hand, by default. If you need a shop or a team updating content daily, we choose the simplest tool that fits and set it up properly.

Can we update it ourselves?+

Yes. Either a simple editor for the parts that change, or a monthly hour where we do it for you.

Who writes the words?+

You know the business; we know what reads well on a screen. Usually both of us.

Where does it live?+

Fast static hosting is often free. We set it up in your accounts, not ours, with the domain in your name.
