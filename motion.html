02 / Live on the GPU

Drawn fresh,
every frame.

Four pieces your graphics card renders as you watch, with WebGPU. Move over them, press, change them. Where WebGPU isn’t available, each one shows a still.

Liquid metal

The mark, pulled
out of mercury.

The unicorn becomes a signed-distance shape, and light is traced across it every frame. Drops of mercury orbit and fuse with it wherever they touch. Bring your pointer in to add one of your own.

Where it fits: a hero, a launch, a product reveal

Letters that grow

Type that
grows itself.

A reaction-diffusion simulation runs inside the letters. Change two numbers and the same word grows as a maze, loops or spots. Touch the letters to seed new growth.

Where it fits: logo stings, loaders, title cards

Ink in water

Stir it
with your hand.

A fluid solver carries three inks from the studio palette through dark water. Two brushes drift on their own; your pointer is the third. Press to change colour.

Where it fits: backgrounds that respond, event pages, an interactive hero

One film, three printers

One asset,
restyled live.

The unicorn film runs through three print processes as it plays: a one-bit dither, a two-ink halftone and ASCII type. One source becomes a whole family of brand moments.

Where it fits: campaign variations, posters, a terminal mode

03 / Built in Blender

Broken on purpose.
Rebuilt in seconds.

A tile carrying the unicorn is cut into 150 shards and struck. Blender’s physics solver works out where every piece falls; we render it and play it backwards, so the mark puts itself together.

06 / How we use motion

Five promises
we keep.

The difference between a site that feels made and one that feels generated is restraint. These are the rules we build to.

  1. 01

    It has a job.

    Every movement points somewhere: what changed, where to look, what happens next.

  2. 02

    Your hand stays in charge.

    We keep native scrolling and the native cursor. Motion answers you; it never takes over.

  3. 03

    Nothing depends on hover.

    Phones and keyboards get the same content and the same controls, with or without the effect.

  4. 04

    Still is always an option.

    Reduced-motion settings and the switch in our footer show the finished frame. Nothing is hidden.

  5. 05

    One signature per page.

    One moment people remember. Everything around it stays quiet.
