    Spring cleanup procedureProcedure · Crews · February 16, 2026+

    A spring cleanup is one visit by a two-person crew. Clear leaves, sticks and winter debris from the beds and the lawn. Cut back perennials and ornamental grasses to four inches. Edge every bed along walks and drives. Prune only dead or broken wood; shaping shrubs is a separate job on the price sheet. Haul all debris away the same day. A standard lot takes three to four crew hours. Before leaving, the crew lead walks the property with the customer or leaves a door tag, then logs the visit with before and after photos.
