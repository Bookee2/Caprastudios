A simulation on sample data. Nothing is sent anywhere.

One customer email, eight steps, one approval.

A customer asks Tallgrass Landscaping, our invented company, to move a visit and what aeration costs. Step through what an agent does with the email, where it waits for a person, and what it writes down.

  1. The email arrives

    It lands in the shared Tallgrass inbox on Thursday, April 9, 2026.

    From
    Ngozi Okafor
    To
    hello@tallgrass.example
    Received
    Thursday, April 9, 2026, 7:42 a.m.
    Subject
    Moving our spring cleanup

    Hi,

    We have a spring cleanup booked for Tuesday the 14th in the morning. We will be away until Wednesday night. Could you come Thursday or Friday that week instead?

    Also, what would it cost to aerate the lawn?

    Thanks,
    Ngozi

  2. The agent reads it

    It finds two separate requests and keeps the customer’s own words beside each one.

    • Move the spring cleanup booked for Tuesday, April 14.Could you come Thursday or Friday that week instead?
    • Give a price for aeration.what would it cost to aerate the lawn?

  3. It looks things up

    Each fact names the document it came from.

    • Client record: Okafor residenceSpring cleanup booked for Tuesday, April 14, morning block, with Dana’s crew.The lot is 8,400 square feet.
    • Scheduling and reschedule policyA customer can move a visit at no charge with two business days’ notice.The visit is three business days away, so there is no fee.
    • 2026 residential price sheetCore aeration: $140. Aeration with overseeding: $245.The sheet covers lots up to 10,000 square feet, so it applies here.
    • Crew calendarThe live schedule, not a document. Dana’s crew is open on Thursday, April 16, from 12:30 to 4:30 p.m.

  4. It drafts a reply and a calendar change

    Both are proposals. Nothing has gone out and the calendar has not changed.

    Draft reply to Ngozi Okafor

    Hi Ngozi,

    Yes, we can move your spring cleanup. We have you down for Thursday, April 16, between 12:30 and 4:30 p.m., with Dana’s crew. There is no charge for the change.

    Core aeration for your lawn is $140. With overseeding it is $245. If you would like either one, reply here and we will book it.

    Thanks,
    Marcus Bell
    Tallgrass Landscaping

  5. It rates each action for risk

    The rules are set before the agent runs. It cannot change them.

    • Send the reply to the customerNeeds approvalIt contacts a customer.
    • Move the booking on the calendarNeeds approvalIt changes a customer’s booking.
    • File the email under the client recordAllowed aloneIt stays inside the company and can be undone.

  6. A person decides

    The agent waits here. In this demo you stand in for Marcus, the office manager.

    In this walk-through, Marcus approves the reply and the calendar change as written.

  7. It carries out what was approved

    • Reply sent to Ngozi Okafor.
    • Booking moved to Thursday, April 16, 12:30 to 4:30 p.m.
    • Email filed under the client record.

    Simulated. Nothing was sent.
