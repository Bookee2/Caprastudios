Purple Squirrel / Founder-built venture

A better kind
of handoff.

A clear, considered application for the journey from candidate submission to client review.

Product design / Web application / Client portal

Product design study

The idea

The rare find deserves a clear introduction.

Staffing teams need to move candidate information between recruiters, reviewers, and clients. Purple Squirrel gives that handoff a coherent interface and a recognizable place to happen.

01 /

Make the candidate easy to understand.

The public product presentation puts candidate context, role, and review status into a readable hierarchy. The important information stays together.

02 /

Give each step a clear place.

Submittals, internal approvals, and client review are distinct parts of the experience. The application organizes the handoff around those roles.

03 /

Carry the identity through.

A consistent visual language connects the product website and the portal experience. Clear states and structured layouts support the work behind the presentation.
