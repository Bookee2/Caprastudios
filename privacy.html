Privacy, plainly.

Capra Studios is a studio operated by Capra, LLC. This page explains what happens to information when you use this website or get in touch.

Visiting the website

This website does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, account registration, or advertising cookies. The assistant keeps its current conversation in page memory; it does not save the transcript in browser storage.

The site remembers two display choices in your browser: the motion effects switch and the black light switch. They stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

The website is designed to be hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider processes request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which also receives technical request information when your browser requests those files.

Getting in touch

Contact links open your email application. Nothing is submitted automatically. If you email the studio, the details you choose to send are used to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. Please don’t include passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information in an initial inquiry.

The studio assistant

The prepared studio guide uses public service information. When live AI is available, it is labeled “AI studio assistant.” Questions and recent conversation context are sent through the studio’s server to Anthropic to generate an answer. The studio does not persist these chat transcripts in its application. The hosting and AI providers may process and retain request information under their own policies. Please avoid sharing confidential or sensitive information in chat.

Project briefs

You can prepare a brief and open it as an email draft. Nothing is emailed automatically. When online submission is available, clicking “Submit project brief” sends the contact information and project details you have reviewed to the studio’s server for follow-up. This requires your permission to be contacted. A brief is an inquiry, not a booking or quote. Email info@caprastudios.co to ask about information you submitted.

Other websites

The portfolio links take you to separate websites. Their services and privacy practices are described on those websites.

Questions

For questions about information you have shared with Capra Studios, email info@caprastudios.co.
