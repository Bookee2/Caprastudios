Privacy, plainly.

Capra Studios is a studio operated by Capra, LLC. This page explains what happens to information when you use this website or get in touch.

Visiting the website

This website does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, account registration, or a contact-form database. It does not set its own cookies or store information in your browser.

The website is designed to be hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider processes request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which also receives technical request information when your browser requests those files.

Getting in touch

Contact links open your email application. Nothing is submitted automatically. If you email the studio, the details you choose to send are used to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. Please don’t include passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information in an initial inquiry.

Other websites

The portfolio links take you to separate websites. Their services and privacy practices are described on those websites.

Questions

For questions about information you have shared with Capra Studios, email kris@caprahr.com.
