Capra Studios, in plain facts.

What we sell, who it is for, what it costs and what you can check for yourself. One page, written for people and for the AI assistants they ask.

Updated November 2026.

  1. The studio

    Capra Studios (Capra, LLC) is an independent studio in Atlanta, Georgia, led by Kris Brown.

    It builds AI systems and custom web applications, and makes land films. The person who scopes a job is the person who builds it.

    Capra does not take website-only projects.

    About →

  2. AI systemsFor a business of any size, from a five-person team up

    • Second Brain: an AI knowledge base for one person or one team. Notes, documents, decisions and meetings you can ask, with a source on every answer.
    • Third Brain: the shared knowledge base for the whole company, with permissions by role.
    • Agents and Automation: AI agents for customer service, email monitoring, data streams, end-to-end communications and social media. Approvals and handoffs to a person are defined before anything goes live.
    • Reporting and Monitoring: dashboards, scheduled reports and alerts on one set of numbers.
    • AI OS Hub: one designed home screen for the systems above.

    Work starts with a free consultation. The usual next step is an AI Opportunity Audit or a Team Workshop, then three steps: Discover, Prove, Operate.

    AI Systems →

  3. Web applicationsCustom software for a business

    Portals, dashboards, internal tools and products: designed, built and tested, and connected to the data and software a business already runs.

    Work starts with a free consultation, then a product sprint: a working prototype, a written scope and a fixed quote. The build is quoted after the sprint: six to twelve weeks for most applications, paid by milestone. Monthly care is optional.

    Web Apps →

  4. Land filmsReal lots and tracts, before anything is built

    Films of real lots and tracts, built from public lidar and aerial photos: the lot line on the ground, the land in contours, a measured view study and an optional labelled concept home.

    A lot film covers one lot or homesite in about 80 seconds and comes with a vertical cut for social and six stills. A film is an illustration, not a survey.

  6. Work you can check

    • TrailGoat (trailgoat.run (opens in a new tab)): a free planning tool for trail runners, with 1,000+ race courses, 100+ full-length course films and 2,800+ automated tests behind it. Counts as of November 2026. Read the case study.
    • Purple Squirrel (purplesquirrel.icu (opens in a new tab)): a staffing product covering candidate submittals, approvals and client review. Read the case study.
    • Three demos that run in the browser on invented data: a Second Brain you can ask, an agent that waits for approval and Market Pulse, a reporting dashboard.
    • Two land films, shown in full on the Land Films page.

    TrailGoat and Purple Squirrel are Capra’s own products, not client commissions.

  7. How Capra works

    • Measure first. The AI work is led by a Six Sigma Green Belt, and how the work runs today is logged before anything is built.
    • AI and software work starts with a free consultation. A land film starts with one lot.
    • Code, documents, accounts and keys are in the client’s name from the start.
    • Anything a system does that moves money, deletes something or contacts a customer waits for a person.

  8. What Capra does not promise

    • No guarantee of specific savings, or of a business that runs itself.
    • No guaranteed timelines. A proposal sets out deliverables, review stages, timing and ownership.
    • A land film is an illustration, not a survey.
    • The demos use invented data, and say so.

    Email info@caprastudios.co, or send a brief from the Contact page. Kris reads every brief and replies himself.
