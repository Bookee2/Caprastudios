Before we begin

A few good
questions.

Can we start with just a website or a film?+

Yes. Websites, applications, and motion projects can stand alone or work together. We’ll shape the scope around what you need.

Can you improve something we already have?+

Yes. We’ll look at what works, what gets in the way, and whether a focused update or a full redesign makes sense.

How do you price a project?+

Every project begins with a defined scope and an upfront quote. The proposal sets out deliverables, review stages, timing, and any hosting or third-party costs.

What does the handoff include?+

Your proposal specifies the source files, final media formats, ownership, and support included. Any third-party licenses are identified before work begins.

Do you work outside Atlanta?+

Yes. Atlanta is home, and we collaborate remotely with businesses and creative teams across the United States.
