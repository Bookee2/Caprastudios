Small but mighty.

Capra is an independent studio in Atlanta, led by Kris Brown. We build AI systems and web applications, and we make land films.

You talk to the people doing the work.

Designers, engineers and AI builders come together around your project. There is no account team in between: the person who scopes the job is the person who builds it.

  1. Measure first.

    Our AI work is led by a Six Sigma Green Belt. Before anything is built we log how the work runs today, so the result can be held against a real number.

  2. A small first step, with its price.

    Every line starts with something small you can judge: an audit, a workshop or a product sprint. The price is on the page.

  3. You own it.

    Code, documents, accounts and keys are in your name from the start. You can run what we build without us.

  4. Nothing we can’t source.

    Every number we show you, in a report or on a film, says where it came from.

  5. People approve what matters.

    Anything a system does that moves money, deletes something or contacts a customer waits for a person.

How we work.

Three steps, the same for an AI system or an application. Each ends with something you keep.

  1. Discover

    Map and measure.

    We map how the work runs today, pick one thing to build and measure it before we start. You own the map and the numbers.

  2. Prove

    Build one thing.

    We build it on your real data and test it against a pass mark agreed in advance. It ends on a date, with a yes or a no.

  3. Operate

    Keep it working.

    Monthly checks, a report on quality and cost, and the next piece when this one has earned it.
