Small but mighty.

Capra Studios is an independent land marketing studio in Atlanta, led by Kris Brown. We make marketing films for land: listings, funds and planned projects.

You talk to the person doing the work.

There is no account team in between. The person who scopes your film is the person who builds it, and Kris reads every brief himself.

  1. Your case, your brand.

    A film carries your name, your colors and your words, and ends on your call to action. It is your marketing, not ours.

  2. Nothing we can’t source.

    Every figure on screen says where it came from: the lidar flight, the county’s parcel map, your survey or the public record.

  3. Labeled when it isn’t built.

    A home, a division or a campus that does not exist yet is labeled a concept, or illustrative, for as long as it is on screen.

  4. You know before you commit.

    We check the land against the public records and tell you what the film can show before you agree to anything.

  5. You own the film.

    The film and its cuts are yours. We show one as an example of our work only if you agree.
