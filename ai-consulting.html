Good questions.

What is RAG?+

Retrieval-augmented generation connects an AI assistant to relevant information from a knowledge source. It retrieves useful passages and gives them to the model as context. It is a knowledge-system approach, not necessarily training a new model.

Can an agent take actions in our tools?+

Yes, when the integration and permissions support it. We define which actions it can take, which require approval, and how errors or unusual requests reach a person.

Can you help rethink our whole operation?+

Yes. Consulting can cover the broader operating approach: workflows, knowledge, roles, tools, and a phased implementation roadmap. I build around the business’s actual requirements.

Do we need to replace our current software?+

Not automatically. I first review what you already use and what its integrations allow. The right plan may connect existing tools, add an agent, or build a focused custom application.

How do we know it is helping?+

We agree on a baseline and success criteria before the pilot: useful answers, successful handoffs, time spent, error rates, and operating costs. Measure the results before scaling up.
